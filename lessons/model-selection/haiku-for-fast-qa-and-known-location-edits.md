# Use Haiku for Fast Q&A and Known-Location Edits

## TL;DR

Use Haiku for fast answers to questions about the code, for teaching/explaining something new, and for making quick, precise edits when you already know exactly which file needs the change.

## Why it matters

Haiku's speed makes it well suited to narrow, well-specified tasks where you don't need deep reasoning, just a fast turnaround.

## How to apply

When you know precisely what needs to change and where, or just want a quick explanation of existing code, direct that request to Haiku rather than a heavier model.
