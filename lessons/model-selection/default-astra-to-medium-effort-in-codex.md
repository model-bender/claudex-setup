# Default Astra to Medium Effort in Codex: High and Above Bought Nothing Measurable

## TL;DR

Across seven controlled threads on the same research-and-build task, Astra on medium produced work on par with high, and extra high, max and ultra added time and tokens without a better result.

## Why it matters

Kashef ran low, medium, high, extra high, max, ultra and a Soul-high control on one prompt. Medium finished fastest (30 minutes, ~10M tokens) with a cleaner analysis than low (37 minutes, ~15M). High was marginally better presented but cost ~40 minutes and ~14M tokens. Extra high looked worse under the hood than high despite 45 minutes, and ultra burned ~22M tokens for a page indistinguishable from the others. His conclusion after this and earlier tests: very few cases where max, extra high or ultra is worth the extra time or tokens, and a follow-up prompt on medium usually closes the gap.

## How to apply

Run Astra on medium as the day-to-day default in Codex (Kashef pairs it with fast mode), and escalate to high only for a task you have already seen medium fail. Treat extra high, max and ultra as reserved rather than available. When medium's output is close but thin, spend a follow-up prompt rather than a level.
