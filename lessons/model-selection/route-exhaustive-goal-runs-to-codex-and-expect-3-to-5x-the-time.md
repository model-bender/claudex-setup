# Send Exhaustive 'Check Every Combination' Goals to Codex, Not Claude, and Budget Roughly 3 to 5x the Time and Tokens

## TL;DR

Mark says Claude 5.5 is less aggressive and comprehensive on a /goal, while Codex on the same goal takes three to five times longer with far more tokens.

## Why it matters

Use it deliberately for jobs that really must check every option, not as the default.

## How to apply

Route that class of work to Codex and set a stop clause. This is Mark's experience, not a benchmark.
