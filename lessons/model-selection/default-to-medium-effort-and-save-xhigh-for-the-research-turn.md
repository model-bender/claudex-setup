# Default to Medium Effort and Take More Turns, Saving xhigh for the Opening Research Turn

## TL;DR

Higher effort can cause overthinking or scope expansion rather than better results, so run medium and iterate; use xhigh mainly when researching the state of a feature or bug before settling on a fix.

## Why it matters

The person who studies effort versus accuracy and cost for Claude Code, and pushed for medium as the Opus 5.5 default, says always-on xhigh often hurts ("often it leads to overthinking or scope expansion that can be unhelpful").

## How to apply

Keep /effort at medium for normal work; spend xhigh (or the ultrathink word) on the opening investigation turn of a session, then drop back.

> "I generally think you are better off taking more turns at medium than running at xhigh all the time.", Adam Wolff (@dmwlff), Claude Code engineer (works on model performance for Claude Code)
