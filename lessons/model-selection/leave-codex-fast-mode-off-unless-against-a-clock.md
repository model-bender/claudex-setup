# Leave Codex's Fast Speed Dial on Standard Unless You Are Genuinely Against a Clock

## TL;DR

Codex's per-chat speed dial (standard/fast) is a cost trap separate from the reasoning dial: fast buys speed you rarely need on non-time-critical work and burns your 5-hour and weekly usage windows noticeably quicker, so keep it on standard.

## Why it matters

The fast-mode bill lands on the 5-hour and weekly session budgets, which are the real constraint in daily Codex use. Unlike picking the wrong reasoning level, the speed setting buys nothing on non-time-critical work, so the extra usage drain is pure waste.

## How to apply

Glance at the speed dial alongside the model and reasoning dials before firing a prompt, and leave it on standard by default. Switch to fast only when you are genuinely working against a clock. Check Settings, then rate limits remaining, to see what your 5-hour and weekly windows have left.
