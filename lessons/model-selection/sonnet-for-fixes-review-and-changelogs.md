# Reserve Sonnet for Small Fixes, Review, and Changelogs

## TL;DR

Reserve Sonnet for small fixes, UI tweaks, reviewing someone else's code, and writing changelogs or release-note summaries.

## Why it matters

These are lower-stakes, narrower-scope tasks where Sonnet's speed and cost outweigh Opus's extra capability.

## How to apply

Route quick fixes, minor UI tweaks, third-party code review, and changelog/summary writing to a Sonnet session or subagent instead of Opus.
