# Walk a Skill Down the Model and Effort Ladder to Find the Cheapest One That Still Holds Quality

## TL;DR

Test a skill starting on the strongest, most expensive model. If the next-cheaper tier reproduces the same result, step down again, and once you've landed on a model, walk down its effort level the same way. Stop at the cheapest combination that still clears your quality bar.

## Why it matters

There's no reason to keep paying for the most capable model on a skill once a cheaper one produces the same output; some tasks (a description-and-timestamps summary) need almost nothing, while others (browser-heavy visual work) genuinely need the top tier. Testing empirically instead of assuming avoids both overpaying and under-provisioning.

## How to apply

Run the skill on your strongest available model tier first. Rerun the identical task on the next tier down; if the output holds up, keep stepping down until quality actually drops, then use the tier just above that drop. Once you've picked a model, repeat the same step-down process on its effort setting (high to medium to low), landing on the lightest setting that still meets the bar.
