# /chrome Can Bypass Many Captchas and Bot-Detection Checks, and You Can Jump In to Help It

## TL;DR

The /chrome browser tool can get past a lot of captchas and other anti-bot challenges on its own, and when it can't, you can manually assist it mid-task rather than starting over.

## Why it matters

This makes /chrome viable for research and data-gathering on sites that actively try to block automated access, which is a common blocker for scraping and browser automation otherwise.

## How to apply

Use /chrome for research or scraping tasks on sites with bot-detection or captchas. If it stalls on a challenge, step in and manually complete that step yourself before letting it continue.
