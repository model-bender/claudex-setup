# Start From Anthropic's First-Party Skill Plugins Rather Than Trawling a Marketplace of Thousands

## TL;DR

Before searching skills.sh, install the vetted first-party packs from the plugins browser, for example the small business plugin's 31 ready-made skills, and adapt those rather than evaluating a marketplace one skill at a time.

## Why it matters

The marketplace problem is volume, not quality: there are genuinely good skills on skills.sh with visible install counts and trending lists, but nobody is going to evaluate thousands of them, so the search cost dominates. Anthropic's own plugins are vetted and cover the common cases, and the small business plugin ships 31 skills each with a description of exactly what it does, for example invoice chase (pull everything overdue, draft a chaser per invoice, wait for your send), close month (reconcile, flag mismatches, list what is missing before the accountant sees it), margin analyzer (which jobs and clients actually make money), and Canva creator (take an approved content brief and execute the campaign end to end). Two well-regarded third-party packs are named as worth the exception, Claude SEO (free and open source, 32 skills covering site audits, technical SEO, EEAT content assessment, AI search and local SEO) and Claude Ads (33 skills across 12 platforms).

## How to apply

In the desktop app, go to settings, plugins, browse, and install the first-party pack that matches your work, then adapt individual skills to your systems rather than writing equivalents from scratch. Hover any skill in the browser to read what it actually does before relying on it. Note the tension with curating installs: a 31-skill pack still loads 31 descriptions into context, so audit afterwards and remove what you never invoke. Treat marketplace browsing as the fallback for genuinely uncovered needs, not the starting point.
