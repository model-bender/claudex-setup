# Disable Auto-Update on a Plugin Marketplace to Pin a Version

## TL;DR

You can disable automatic updating for a specific plugin marketplace to keep an older, known-good version of it pinned on your machine.

## Why it matters

A marketplace can push updates that change or break a plugin you rely on; pinning avoids being surprised by that.

## How to apply

In /plugins, find the marketplace's settings and turn off automatic updating to freeze it at its current version.
