# Have Claude fill a context folder from your site, files, email and drive instead of writing it by hand

## TL;DR

Ask Claude (in plan mode first) to build files about you, the business, customers, numbers and writing voice from existing material: your website, local folders, Gmail and Google Drive.

## Why it matters

The speaker's before and after: the same question about getting 10 more leads gets a generic answer without context ('I don't know your business from this message alone') and a specific one after, naming what already works (venue referrals at 40%) and an offer with no outbound effort behind it (corporate lunch). Doing this by hand would be a very large job.

## How to apply

Connect Gmail and Drive as connectors on claude.ai, switch to plan mode, then prompt along the lines of the video: plan how to fill the context folder from our website, the files on our computer, Gmail and Google Drive, and do not take action yet. When the plan reads only a project folder (the output folder, in the video), widen local sources to Documents, Downloads and Desktop, accept, and let it write files such as about-me, business, customers, numbers, voice, competitors and gaps.

The prompt is paraphrased from the transcript, not quoted. The demo uses a sample business, so the quality of the after answer is illustrative.
