# Drag a Codex Thread Into a New Thread to Seed It With a Reference, Not a Full Copy

## TL;DR

In the Codex app you can drag an existing thread into another so the new one references its context and starts fresh, which differs from branching a chat (that creates a worktree).

## Why it matters

John Kim says it is a Codex-only way to reuse another thread's context without carrying the whole of it. He demonstrates by asking 'what was this about' in the seeded thread.

## How to apply

1. Open the new thread. 2. Drag the source thread from the sidebar into it. 3. Ask what you need. Use 'branch the chat' instead when you want a worktree. Checked: app UI behaviour is the speaker's, not verifiable against codex-cli 0.163.0.
