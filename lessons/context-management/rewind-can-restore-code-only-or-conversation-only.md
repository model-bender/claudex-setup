# Rewind Lets You Restore Just the Code, Just the Conversation, or Both

## TL;DR

When picking a checkpoint to rewind to, Claude Code offers separate options to restore only the codebase state, only the conversation history, or both together.

## Why it matters

Sometimes you want to undo a bad edit but keep the discussion that led to a good insight, or the reverse; a single combined rewind would throw one of them away.

## How to apply

Open the rewind/checkpoint menu, pick the point you want, then choose whether to restore just the code, just the conversation, or both.
