# Do not force one template on decision artifacts

## TL;DR

Let each briefing artifact be designed for the situation at hand rather than filling a fixed template.

## Why it matters

Different decisions need different evidence. The speaker's auth artifact carried a mockup and tested user flows, while another carried proof of the feature running and screenshots from the application.

## How to apply

Specify what a briefing must let you decide, not its layout, and let the agent choose the visuals that fit.
