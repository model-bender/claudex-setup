# Watch for Codex's Colored Dot on a Chat to Know It Needs Your Attention

## TL;DR

Codex marks a project's chats in the sidebar with a small blue or yellow dot when that background session needs a response or has finished, so you can multitask across several chats.

## Why it matters

With several chats running in one project, polling each tab wastes time; the dot lets you glance at the sidebar and know which session to check.

## How to apply

Open multiple chats within a Codex project for different tasks, and watch the left-hand chat list for the colored dot indicator instead of switching between tabs to check status.
