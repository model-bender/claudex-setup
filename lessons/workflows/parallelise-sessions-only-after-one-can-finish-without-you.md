# Parallelise sessions only after a single session can finish without you

## TL;DR

Do not fan out to parallel sessions until one session can complete the task without your intervention.

## Why it matters

The article read in the video says only that parallel sessions pay off once a single session can finish without you. This lesson's reading of that line: if each session still needs you, running several multiplies the babysitting rather than the output.

## How to apply

First get one session to completion unattended (clear goal, a check it can run). Then add sessions.
