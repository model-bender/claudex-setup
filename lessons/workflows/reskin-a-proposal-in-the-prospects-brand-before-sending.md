# Re-skin Your Proposal in the Prospect's Own Branding Before You Send It

## TL;DR

Take a proposal you already have, point Claude Design at the prospect's company as the brand source, and have it re-emit the same document in their palette, type and style.

## Why it matters

This is the inverse of building your own design system, and Marchese frames it as the cheapest trust signal in a sales cycle: handing someone a proposal that looks like their own company made it shows you went the extra mile. The document's content and section order are already proven, so only the skin changes, which is exactly the layer Claude Design is good at swapping.

## How to apply

Attach your existing proposal as the structure source and name the target company (its site or brand kit) as the brand source in the same prompt, making the division of labour explicit. Review the generated version for their logo being redrawn and for type that drifted, then send.
