# Connect the Repo So Claude Design Maps Your Real Funnel, Not a Generic One

## TL;DR

Attach your project or codebase to Claude Design and ask it to break down your funnel: it reads the actual components people click and visualises each stage, then generates conversion variants per stage.

## Why it matters

Asked cold, Claude Design produces a textbook funnel diagram that tells you nothing about your product. With the code connected it maps the real steps and the specific design components that drive each transition, which is the difference between a diagram and an audit. Marchese then asked for two variants of each stage optimised for conversion, so the whole funnel could be optimised as one system rather than one page at a time.

## How to apply

Connect the project or repo in Claude Design, then prompt it to break down your product's funnel. Follow with a request for two designs at each stage optimised for conversion so you can compare variants side by side, and pick per stage.
