# Type the Final Copy on the Canvas Instead of Prompting Claude Design for Micro-Edits

## TL;DR

When the design is right but the words are not yours, edit the text in place on the design rather than spending prompt rounds asking for wording tweaks.

## Why it matters

Marchese names the failure mode 'doom prompting': you go back and forth a few times, it still is not how you would say it, you give up and export AI slop. Because the design tool lets you edit text directly, the last 10% (the part that decides whether a reader can tell AI wrote it) costs zero rounds and stays in your voice. This is the difference he draws between a customer-ready document and something the recipient stops reading.

## How to apply

Generate the layout by prompt, then click into the text on the canvas and rewrite it as you would say it. Note the current value before editing, because direct canvas edits have no undo. Reserve prompts for structural or visual changes.
