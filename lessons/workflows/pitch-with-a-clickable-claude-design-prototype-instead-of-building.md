# Pitch With a Clickable Claude Design Prototype Instead of Building the Product

## TL;DR

For validating or selling a product idea, generate a clickable Claude Design wireframe you can walk someone through, and stop there rather than having Claude Code build it.

## Why it matters

Marchese used exactly this at his last startup when pitching custom products to artists: the buyer clicks through something that looks and feels like the product without any backend work. His honest caveat is that Claude Code makes building easier but not easy, so for an idea that may not survive the pitch, a wireframe is often the entire deliverable. This is the lowest-cost artifact that still answers 'do you want this'.

## How to apply

In Claude Design, describe the product and ask for wireframes with working navigation between screens, answering its question round or handing it over with 'decide for me'. Click through the flow yourself first, then pitch from it. Promote it to a real build only after the idea survives.
