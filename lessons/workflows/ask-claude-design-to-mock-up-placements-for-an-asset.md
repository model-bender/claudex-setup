# When You Don't Know How to Use an Asset, Ask Claude Design to Mock Up Placements For It

## TL;DR

After Claude Design produces an asset you like but have no plan for, ask it to show the asset in context on your site in several options, and pick the placement rather than inventing one.

## Why it matters

Marchese had it model a 3D version of his logo, loved it, and had no idea what to do with a spinning logo. Prompting 'can you put this on a website in the different options that we have' returned three realistic placements and he picked the slow turntable. The useful move is leaning on the tool's taste for the placement question, which is the part a non-designer is worst at, instead of leaving a good asset unused.

## How to apply

Once an asset is generated, prompt for placements in context rather than more variations of the asset: 'put this on the site in the different options we have.' Compare the mockups, choose one, and only then hand the chosen composition to code.
