# Pick High Fidelity, Not Wireframe, When You Want a Near-Final Claude Design Prototype

## TL;DR

Claude Design's prototype step asks for Wireframe or High Fidelity; Wireframe gives a rough unstyled mock-up, High Fidelity applies your published design system directly for an almost production-ready page.

## Why it matters

Picking the wrong fidelity wastes a generation: Wireframe output still needs a full styling pass, while High Fidelity uses your design system's colors, buttons and type immediately.

## How to apply

When creating a prototype in Claude Design, after selecting your published design system, choose 'High Fidelity' (not 'Wireframe') if the goal is a site you can hand to Claude Code largely as-is.
