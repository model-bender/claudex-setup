# Claude Design Lives at claude.ai/design, and the Desktop App Now Opens It From a Design Button

## TL;DR

Claude Design is an Anthropic Labs project at claude.ai/design. It launched web-only (June 2026), but by August 2026 the Claude desktop app has a design button that takes you to it. No source here confirms the mobile app.

You need at least the $20/month Pro plan to get access to Claude Design; free-tier accounts can't use it.

## Why it matters

The June source states it plainly: 'It's important to know that it's not in the Claude desktop or mobile app yet, but it is on the web, which means it's actually an Anthropic Labs project, which means that it's experimental.'

That changed within two months. The August source, walking through the desktop app, points at 'this design button down here. This will take you to Claude design.' So the June advice not to look for it in the desktop app is out of date. Corrected in place on 2026-09-24 (the id keeps its old name so existing links still resolve).

The presenter of the June source states the plan requirement as a prerequisite in his 'what to know before you start' step: 'you do need at least a pro plan, which is $20 a month or higher. If you have that, then you're good to go.'

## How to apply

Open Claude Design at claude.ai/design in a browser, or from the design button in the Claude desktop app. Do not assume the mobile app has it.

Confirm your Claude account is on Pro or higher before trying to access it.
