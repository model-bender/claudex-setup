# Watch for the Agent-Team Leader Implementing Directly Instead of Delegating

## TL;DR

The orchestrator sometimes starts writing code itself rather than assigning it to a teammate, or starts a new task before waiting for teammates to finish their current one.

## Why it matters

This defeats the point of parallelizing across a team and can create work that later conflicts with what a teammate is doing.

## How to apply

If you notice the leader coding directly, interrupt it and say delegate this to your team, or wait for your teammates to finish before starting the next task.
