# Stop relying on the standalone Claude Design site: it now lives in Claude under Artifacts and the old site is closing

## TL;DR

Claude Design now sits inside Claude (Artifacts, or just ask in chat) and the old standalone site goes away in December, so start new work in Claude and move anything you still need.

## Why it matters

Work left on the standalone site becomes unreachable when it closes.
