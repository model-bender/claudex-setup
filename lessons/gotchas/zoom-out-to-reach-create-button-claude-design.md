# Zoom Out in Your Browser When Claude Design's Brief Button Is Pushed Off-Screen by Attachments

## TL;DR

Attaching many reference screenshots to a Claude Design brief pushes the 'describe what you want to create' button off the visible page; zoom out in the browser to reach it rather than assuming the UI is broken.

## Why it matters

This is a small, easy-to-miss UI quirk, if you don't know to zoom out you can end up thinking the create step is missing or stuck when it's just below the fold.

## How to apply

After attaching a large batch of screenshots to a Claude Design brief, use your browser's zoom-out shortcut (Ctrl/Cmd + -) to bring the create button back into view before submitting.
