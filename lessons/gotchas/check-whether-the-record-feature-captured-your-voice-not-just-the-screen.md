# Check the Record Feature Captured Your Voice, Not Just the Screen

## TL;DR

Before relying on a screen-record-to-skill run, confirm what it actually captured: some recorders take the screen without your audio, which loses the narration carrying all of your reasoning.

## Why it matters

The spoken commentary is most of the value of a recording, it is where the tacit 'I do this because' reasoning lives, the part you would never write down. A silent recording still produces a plausible-looking SOP or skill, built purely from what the clicks looked like, so the loss is invisible until the artifact turns out to encode no judgement at all.

## How to apply

Do a short test recording and play it back, or ask the agent what it received (frames only, or frames plus audio), before spending a long session on tape. If the built-in recorder is silent or unavailable in your region, screen-record with any recorder that captures the mic and feed the file in as raw video instead.
