# Claude Design Now Opens on Every Plan, but the Free Plan Tops Out at Sonnet 5.5

## TL;DR

Claude Design came out of beta and is no longer gated behind a paid plan: a free account can open it (Artifacts, then Design) and run a prompt, but Sonnet 5.5 is the highest model there, and heavy work may not fit the free usage.

## Why it matters

The older gotcha said a free-tier account could not open Claude Design at all, which would stop someone from trying it or recommending it. Tristen O'Brien says "Claude Design came out of beta and that means it's open on every single plan", and on the free plan "you get Sonnet 5.5 as the highest model"; picking Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.1 asks for an upgrade. His own verdict on the free plan for his animation was negative: it "probably wouldn't be usable" and you may not have enough usage to finish one video.

## How to apply

Do not rule Claude Design out for a free account, but expect Sonnet 5.5 as the ceiling and a tight usage budget. Move heavier generations that want Opus 5.5 to a Pro or Max account. This rests on one creator's report from October 2026, so confirm in the account's own model picker before planning around it.
