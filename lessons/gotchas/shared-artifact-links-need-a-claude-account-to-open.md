# A Shared Claude Slide-Deck Artifact Link Needs a Claude Account to Open

## TL;DR

Austin warns that when you send someone the link to a slide-deck artifact, they need a Claude account to open it. Slides can be exported to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides instead.

## Why it matters

Austin lists this as a gotcha people miss after landing on a good deck. His artifacts video shows a different case: a micro-tool artifact made publicly accessible from the share menu opens with no login, so the account requirement depends on how the artifact is shared.

## How to apply

Before sending a deck link, check the share setting and whether the recipients have Claude accounts; if not, export the slides to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides.
