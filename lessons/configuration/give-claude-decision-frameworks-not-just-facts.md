# Give Claude Decision Frameworks to Bias Toward, Not Only Facts

## TL;DR

State the bias Claude should lean on when choices are open, such as simplest best solution over most scalable, or clarity over creativity.

## Why it matters

Austin Marchese's takeaway from an Anthropic engineers' video: context is not only facts, it is the framework that decides trade-offs. The right answer changes with, for example, one user versus 100,000 users.

## How to apply

Write one or two bias lines where Claude will read them (the video names no file; CLAUDE.md or project instructions are this lesson's suggestion): for code, 'bias towards the simplest best solution, not necessarily the most scalable one'; for writing, 'bias towards clarity over creativity'.
