# Import a Pre-Built Brand Kit From Get Design Into Claude Design

## TL;DR

Get Design (getdesign.com) publishes free design.md brand-kit files for dozens of major brands; paste one into Claude Design's notes to seed a design system instantly instead of relying only on screenshots.

## Why it matters

Screenshots alone leave Claude Design guessing at exact values; in the video, rounded buttons only showed up because the design.md text kit specified them, not the Stripe screenshots. A maintained text kit gives Claude Design authoritative values screenshots can't convey.

## How to apply

Find the brand's page on Get Design (or a similar free brand-kit site), open its design.md file, copy the full text. In Claude Design, create a Design System, upload your own screenshots for assets, then paste the design.md text into the 'any other notes' field before generating.
