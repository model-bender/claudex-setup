# Budget 10-15 Minutes and Real Quota for Claude Design's Design-System Ingestion

## TL;DR

Creating a Claude Design design system (brand name, repo, files, logos) takes roughly 10-15 minutes to ingest into a spec, and that ingestion alone burns a meaningful chunk of quota.

## Why it matters

The creator notes that once you feed a design system all its inputs, "it has to ingest it, and it has to create that actual spec... this can eat a decent amount of tokens, because it's going to take like 10 to 15 minutes," a real cost separate from the prototypes built afterward.

## How to apply

Treat design-system creation as its own budgeted step (about 10-15 minutes, real quota) rather than assuming it's instant; build it once per brand, then export and reuse it instead of recreating it per project.
