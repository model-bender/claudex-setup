# Don't Poll Agent-Team Teammates: the Lead Is Notified Automatically When a Task Completes

## TL;DR

You never have to manually check on individual teammates for completion; the team lead receives an automatic notification when a teammate finishes its task, so you can just watch the lead's pane.

## Why it matters

Manually tabbing through every teammate pane to check status wastes time the automatic notification already covers.

## How to apply

Keep your attention on the orchestrator/lead pane; only jump into a teammate's pane to steer it mid-task, not to poll for completion.
