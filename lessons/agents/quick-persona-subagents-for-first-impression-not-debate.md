# Spin Up Persona Subagents for a Quick Gut-Check, Not a Full Debate

## TL;DR

When you just need signal on messaging or design, tell persona subagents to give only their first impression, explicitly skipping debate or deep analysis.

## Why it matters

The creator was explicit about scoping this down: 'spin up a few different agent personas, business owners, agency owners, small business owners, CEOs, and I want you to have them read the messaging... You don't have to have them debate. You don't have to have them give you a deep analysis. Just tell me their first impression.' This is a lighter-weight variant of a full multi-agent debate panel, useful when you want fast directional signal rather than a resolved decision.

## How to apply

When you want quick validation rather than a decision, prompt: 'spin up N persona subagents representing [roles], have each give a one-paragraph first impression only, no debate, no deep critique', then read the impressions yourself rather than asking for a synthesized verdict.
