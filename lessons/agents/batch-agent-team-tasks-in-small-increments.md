# Hand Each Agent-Team Batch Only 5-6 Tasks, Then Regroup Before the Next Batch

## TL;DR

Even when you've planned a long list of subtasks up front, dole them out to the team in small batches of about 5-6, bring everyone back, then kick off the next batch rather than releasing the whole backlog at once.

## Why it matters

Releasing too much work at once makes it harder to steer and review before mistakes compound across the team.

## How to apply

When directing an agent team, explicitly cap each round at 5-6 tasks, e.g. let's do five tasks or so at a time, even if a much longer plan already exists.
