# Have Claude Interview You With a 10-20 Question Brand-Voice Quiz for a Fast Starting Profile

## TL;DR

The fastest way to bootstrap a brand-voice profile from zero is to have Claude ask you 10-20 questions about how you talk and what you'd never say, rather than starting from scraped examples.

## Why it matters

Scraping historical posts requires you to already have a body of writing to mine; the quiz works even with no existing content and gives a usable rule set in one sitting.

## How to apply

Ask Claude to interview you with 10-20 questions covering tone, phrases you'd never use, and style preferences, then have it compile the answers into your brand-voice file. Refine later by scraping real examples (LinkedIn, email, community replies) per platform.
