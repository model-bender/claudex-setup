# After a Big Change, Ask 'How Can I Manually Verify This?' and Get a Personal Verification Plan

## TL;DR

Austin says most people still verify by hand but neglect to have the AI help with that step, so he asks it for a manual verification plan.

## Why it matters

Default AI self-checks are generic because the AI does not know what correct looks like for your task.

## How to apply

After any big change, prompt: 'How can I manually verify this?' Follow the plan it hands back.
