# In Codex, Prefer Natural-Language Context Over @-Mentions

## TL;DR

When prompting Codex and you don't already know the exact file, describe the goal in plain language rather than typing an @-mention, and let Codex semantically find the right context.

## Why it matters

The presenter explicitly recommends against over-using @ in Codex prompts, saying natural-language search finds the right context as well as, or better than, guessing at a path.

## How to apply

Reserve @-mentions in Codex for when you already know precisely which file you mean; otherwise describe what you need in natural language and let Codex locate the relevant files itself.
