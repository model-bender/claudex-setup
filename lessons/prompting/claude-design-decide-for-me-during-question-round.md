# Answer Claude Design's Question Round With 'Decide for Me' to Skip Manual Choices

## TL;DR

When Claude Design's animation/video wizard asks a batch of setup questions (talking-head placement, visual energy, motion graphics style), you can just reply 'decide for me' and it picks sensible defaults instead of you specifying each one.

When Claude Design asks its pre-build clarifying questions (audience, vibe, colors, section count), you can answer 'decide for me' and it will make every remaining choice itself instead of making you answer each one.

## Why it matters

Manually answering every question up front slows you down before you've even seen a draft; deferring to Claude Design's judgment gets a first pass fast, and you can give targeted feedback on the actual output instead of guessing blind.

The presenter demonstrates this directly: after Claude Design asks a batch of questions about the gumball-machine landing page, he says 'you can answer these yourselves if you'd like, or you can say, if you don't know, you can just say decide for me, and then Claude's going to handle the entire thing.'

## How to apply

When prompted with the multi-part questionnaire in Claude Design (e.g. 'where does your talking head sit', 'visual energy level', 'motion graphics style'), reply 'decide for me' for any question you don't have a strong opinion on; only answer where you actually care.

When Claude Design's question round appears, reply 'decide for me' (for all or specific questions) to let it choose sensible defaults and proceed straight to building.
