# Answer who it is for, what they get and what they do next, and paste that before asking for a website design

## TL;DR

AI writes generic copy; write the three first-screen answers yourself (who is this for, the outcome not the feature, one call to action) and give them to Claude before any design request.

## Why it matters

The transcript's mirror test says the right visitor should feel they are looking in a mirror. It cites a trucker job-site page that converted 79.3% better after being rebuilt around the driver, and a headline change that lifted pricing clicks 90% (figures are the creator's, not verified).

## How to apply

Write three lines: who the page is for (use the words they type into Google), what they get (an outcome such as fill your calendar without answering DMs, not AI-powered scheduling), and the single next action (book your free call). Paste them into Claude before asking for design.
