# Rebuild a Reference Site in Your Brand by Handing Claude Design the Gallery's Prompt or URL

## TL;DR

Motion and component galleries publish the prompt that produced each effect. Copy it into Claude Design and add "use my design system, swap in my background video, rewrite the copy for my brand." Claude Design can also fetch a URL and browse the linked GitHub repo itself.

## Why it matters

This is how a full site got rebuilt as a 3D scroll journey in 20 minutes: take a motionsites.ai scroll-journey prompt wholesale and substitute your own video, colors, and copy.

**The hard part of a striking effect is the technique, not the content**, and these galleries hand you the technique as text.

And you don't always have to copy: pasting a catalog URL had Design fetch the page, browse the repo, and pull a phone-animation scene into an existing video.

## How to apply

Browse motionsites.ai (backgrounds and full scroll-journey sites, mostly free), godly.website, awwwards, or 21st.dev for individual components.

Hit copy on the prompt and paste it into Claude Design with the substitutions spelled out:

> This is a cool concept. Replace the background video with this one, and update all the copy and brand colors to my brand.

Plus your design system. Or just paste the catalog page URL and let Design fetch it.

Open Design ships the same idea natively as an examples gallery with a per-example prompt.
