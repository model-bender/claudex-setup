# Give a /goal a Rubric, a Finish Line, and a 'Stop and Tell Me Why After 2 Hours' Clause

## TL;DR

Mark's example: 'get the checkout tests passing', a finish line, and 'After 2 hours, if you're stuck, stop and tell me why', or stop and tell the other model.

## Why it matters

He says Codex can spend 18 to 30 hours and loop on the same situations, especially with computer use, using the entire token budget.

## How to apply

Write the goal with a measurable rubric, a finish line, and a time-boxed stop-and-report clause.
