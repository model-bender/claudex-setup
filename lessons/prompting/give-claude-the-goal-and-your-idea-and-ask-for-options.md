# Hand Claude the Goal Plus Your Idea and Ask for Options, Not the Solution as an Instruction

## TL;DR

Say what you want to improve, offer your idea as one example, and ask what to do; a solution-shaped prompt caps the result at your answer.

## Why it matters

Austin Marchese describes a Claude Code engineer who stopped asking Claude to build his idea and instead asked it to improve a funnel and come up with ideas, giving his own as an example. His argument: given an answer, the best Claude can do is that answer; given a goal, it can return options you did not think of, with your original idea still on the table.

## How to apply

Rewrite task prompts as goals: 'Improve my newsletter sign-up page' instead of 'Change the button to blue'; 'Suggest ways to improve the client proposal' instead of 'Add pricing table on page three'. Add 'here is one idea, what should we do?'. Keep precise tasks for work that really is a fixed task; the creator says doing both is fine.
