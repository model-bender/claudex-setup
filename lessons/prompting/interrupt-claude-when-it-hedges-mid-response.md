# Interrupt Claude the Moment It Says 'I'm Not Really Sure'

## TL;DR

Treat hedging language or visible uncertainty in Claude's thinking/output as a trigger to interrupt (Escape) and course-correct rather than letting it guess.

## Why it matters

John Kim explicitly calls out watching for phrases like 'I'm not really sure' or signs it's making unverified assumptions as the cue to stop it early, rather than waiting to see if the guess turns out wrong.

## How to apply

While Claude is working, skim its thinking/response for hedge words or assumption-flagging language; the moment you see it, press Escape and give it the missing information instead of letting it proceed on a guess.
