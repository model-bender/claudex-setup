# Ask Fable 5.2 What Is Wrong With Your Idea Before It Builds Anything

## TL;DR

Make the first turn a critique of your plan ("what's wrong with this idea?") before any code is written.

## Why it matters

The model will push back on a flawed plan, and that is cheaper than building the wrong thing. The same post notes it does well on "big, open-ended work".

## How to apply

Open the task with "What's wrong with this idea? Don't build anything yet", then proceed once you have answered the objections.

> "Ask what’s wrong with your idea before it builds anything. It’s great at brainstorming and will push back", Alex Albert (Anthropic) on X
