# The / Menu Lists Your Skills Ordered by How Often You Use Them

## TL;DR

Opening the / menu shows your installed skills sorted by usage frequency, most-used first, rather than alphabetically or by install order.

## Why it matters

With many skills installed, frequency ordering surfaces the ones you actually reach for at the top, cutting down on scrolling or typing the full name.

## How to apply

Type / and look at the skills section: it's already ordered by how frequently you've invoked each one, so your common skills sit near the top.
