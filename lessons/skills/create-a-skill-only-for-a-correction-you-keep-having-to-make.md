# Create a Skill Only for Something You Keep Sending Back, and Mine Your History to Find It

## TL;DR

Matt Pocock's definition: a skill is a correction you got tired of making. If the model already gets the task right first time, the skill is just token cost.

## Why it matters

The video's rule: stop asking what you used AI for, ask what you had to send back and which step of the process it was. Skills for things the model already does right cost tokens for nothing.

## How to apply

Have Claude scan your last 30 days of conversation history for places you sent a result back, and list them as skill candidates, each tied to one step. The video shows a prompt for this but does not give its text, so write your own.
