# Scope Each Skill to a Single Step of the Process to Avoid Premature Completion

## TL;DR

Models rush toward producing the asset. A skill that covers one step, such as research or outline or revision, resists that.

## Why it matters

Matt Pocock calls it an 'asset rush', a premature completion where the model is trained to finish and produce something right now. The video's author advises not to rush end to end and, for content work, to break the job into research, outline, scripting and revisions, each its own problem, and to aim for middle-to-middle rather than end-to-end.

## How to apply

Split an end-to-end skill into one skill per step, and invoke them in turn. Ask what step of the process each correction came from.
