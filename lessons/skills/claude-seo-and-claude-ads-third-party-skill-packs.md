# Use the Free Claude SEO and Claude Ads Skill Packs Instead of Building SEO/Ads Skills From Scratch

## TL;DR

A free, open-source third-party skill pack called Claude SEO offers 32 skills for technical SEO, content ranking, and AI/geo search audits, and a companion Claude Ads pack offers 33 skills across 12 ad platforms.

## Why it matters

These are comprehensive, already-built alternatives to hand-rolling SEO or ad-management skills, covering things like full site audits, Google E-E-A-T assessment, and platform-specific ad creation and auditing.

## How to apply

Search for 'Claude SEO' and 'Claude Ads' (by creator Daniel) and install them as skill packs rather than building equivalent skills yourself; the packs cover full-site technical audits, EEAT content scoring, local/geo SEO, and multi-platform ad auditing and creation.
