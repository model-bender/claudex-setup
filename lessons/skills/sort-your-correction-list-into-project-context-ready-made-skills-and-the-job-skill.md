# Before building a skill, sort your list of corrections into context, existing skills and the job's own rules

## TL;DR

Do the job once without a skill, have Claude keep a list of every correction you make, then sort that list: facts about you or your business go in project context, generic AI habits go to an existing skill, and only the rest forms the new skill.

## Why it matters

The video's rule is that a skill should hold only the fixes Claude cannot get right out of the box; mixing context and generic style fixes into every skill duplicates them.

## How to apply

Run the task, ask Claude to log each correction, then sort: about you or your business means CLAUDE.md or project context; generic phrasing habits (the video's example is the 'it's not X, it's Y' opener) means an installed humanizing skill such as Stop Slop; what is left is job-specific and becomes the skill body. Add real examples of good output.
