# Before Writing a Skill, Ask Claude Whether an Established Term or Framework Already Names What You Want

## TL;DR

A word the model already knows deeply replaces paragraphs of explanation. A made-up word recruits no priors.

## Why it matters

Matt Pocock's quoted line: a made-up word recruits no priors, you pay in definition tokens what a pre-trained word gives free. Their grill-me skill leans on words like 'relentlessly', 'decision tree' and 'frontier'. The video suggests non-technical leading words too: SMART goals, steelman, premortem, BLUF.

## How to apply

Ask Claude: 'is there an established framework or term for what I am describing?' and use its word in the skill in place of your own description. Also ask it to audit your existing skills for missing leading words.

Verified: Prompting practice, no CLI mechanism. Claude Code 2.1.287.
