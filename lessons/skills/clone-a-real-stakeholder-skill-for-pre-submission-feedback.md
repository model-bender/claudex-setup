# Build 'Clone-the-Reviewer' Skills From a Real Person's Own Writing to Pre-Review Your Work

## TL;DR

For non-code deliverables, build a skill that clones the specific real person who will judge the work, your actual manager (from her blog posts, Slack messages, emails), the end user/customer, or named vertical experts, from their real artifacts, so you get their likely feedback and can revise before you ever submit.

## Why it matters

As production becomes free, quality is the moat; a persona cloned from a real reviewer's own words gives targeted, in-their-voice critique ('would Hormozi approve this sales email?', 'what would my manager flag?') that generic 'make it better' prompts and abstract personas can't, surfacing the objection before it reaches the person who matters.

## How to apply

Scrape a stakeholder's available content and have Claude build an activatable skill that critiques a draft as that individual: ask-the-board (4-5 vertical thought leaders), internal-focus-group (the actual end user), or clone-your-manager (your boss's writing). Run it as an approval gate before sending. Distinct from a generic decision panel [[agent-team-debate-panel-for-decisions]] and a self-clone another lesson, here you clone the specific person you must satisfy.
