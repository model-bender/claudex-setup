# Check Codex's Rate Limits Remaining Panel for 5-Hour and Weekly Usage

## TL;DR

Settings > Rate Limits Remaining shows two separate percentages: how much of your rolling 5-hour session budget is left, and how much of your weekly budget is left.

## Why it matters

Knowing both numbers lets you decide whether to keep using a high reasoning effort or throttle down before you exhaust either window.

## How to apply

Open Settings and click 'Rate limits remaining' to see the 5-hour reset percentage and the separate weekly reset percentage.
