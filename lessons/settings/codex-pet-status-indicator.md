# Use Codex's Pet Icon to Track Background Task Status While You Multitask

## TL;DR

Codex shows a small on-screen pet (chosen in Settings > Appearance > Pets) that indicates when it's actively working, letting you glance at progress without switching back to the app.

## Why it matters

Hovering the pet, or just noticing it's animated, tells you Codex is still working on a task in another tab or window, which is useful when running several chats or working elsewhere on your screen.

## How to apply

Go to Settings > Appearance > Pets to pick a skin (Codex's default, Dewey, CD, Beasod, Stacky, Fireball, etc). Run /pet to hide it, hover it to see its current task while it's animated.
