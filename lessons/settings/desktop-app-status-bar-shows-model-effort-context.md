# The Claude Desktop App's Built-In Status Bar Already Shows Model, Effort, and Context Percentage

## TL;DR

The Claude desktop app has a status bar (right side) that natively displays the current model, effort level, and context-window percentage, no custom statusline setup required.

## Why it matters

The presenter points this out directly: 'When you look at the desktop app, on the right side of the bar, you can see the model, the level of effort, and your current context,' and uses it as the basis for deciding when to switch models, lower effort, or compact.

## How to apply

Look at the right side of the status bar in the Claude desktop app to check model/effort/context usage at a glance, rather than assuming you need to configure a custom statusline first.
