# Type Your Next Prompt While Claude Is Still Working: It Queues

## TL;DR

You don't have to wait for Claude to finish before typing the next instruction; Claude Code queues and sequences multiple entered prompts logically.

## Why it matters

John Kim notes people are 'scared to enter more than one prompt' but says Claude Code 'has a really good way of deduping and entering prompts in a nice queue' and 'logically knows what to do,' so queuing prompts (or interrupting and redirecting) is expected usage, not a risk.

## How to apply

Keep typing and submitting follow-up instructions even while Claude is mid-turn; it will queue them and work through them in order, or you can escape to interrupt and redirect instead.
