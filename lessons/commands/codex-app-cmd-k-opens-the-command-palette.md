# Press Cmd+K in the Codex App to Fuzzy-Find Any Setting or Command

## TL;DR

Cmd+K opens a general find/command palette in the Codex app that jumps straight to Settings or runs any available command.

## Why it matters

Codex app settings and actions are spread across menus and side panels, so reaching one means hunting through the UI. The palette replaces that with a single fuzzy search, which matters most for the settings people change often (personality, dictation, connections) and for commands that have no visible button.

## How to apply

In the Codex app press Cmd+K, type part of the setting or command name, and pick it from the filtered list.
